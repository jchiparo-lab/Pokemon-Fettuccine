POKEMON FETTUCCINE VERSION - PLAYTEST 5
=======================================

Thanks for testing! This is a big one.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. You need a clean US Pokemon Emerald ROM (not included).
   It should match:
     CRC32: 1F1C08FB
     SHA-1: f3ae088181bf583e55daf962a92bb46f4f1d07b7
2. Apply fettuccine-playtest-5.bps to it. Either of these works:
     - Rom Patcher JS (in your browser): https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/
     - Floating IPS (Flips)
3. Play the patched ROM in mGBA or any GBA emulator.

Your Playtest 4 save keeps working: give the new ROM the same file
name as before (so the save file matches) or copy the .sav across.
Use an in-game save, not a save state from an older playtest.
The first time you load it, your medicine moves into the new
MEDICINE pocket and your TMs move into the new TM pocket by
themselves. If you already have the Mega Ring, talk to Nurse Joy
to get the new MEGA STONE CASE.


NEW SINCE PLAYTEST 4
--------------------
Getting around
- One BIKE: Rydel gives a single BIKE. Press B while riding to
  switch between FAST mode and ACRO mode. In ACRO mode, the R
  button does wheelies, bunny hops and jumps.
- Wild Pokemon no longer depend on the time of day. Every Pokemon
  in an area shows up at any hour, and nothing is rarer than 4%.
- 21 Pokemon that only appeared evolved (Litten, Popplio,
  Sprigatito, Chimchar and others) now have their first stage in
  an early area.

Bag and Pokedex
- New MEDICINE pocket for Potions, status heals, Revives, vitamins
  and candies.
- The TM pocket now holds every TM and HM.
- Pokedex search: pick TYPE A NAME in the Name list to type a
  Pokemon's name. Search now looks through the National Dex by
  default.

Shops and items
- Every Poke Mart is now RETRO SALVAGE GAMES, run by JASON.
- 50 new TMs (TM51-TM100) with moves from newer games, sold at
  Retro Salvage Games as you go through the story.
- About 270 new items to buy: held items (Choice items, Life Orb,
  Focus Sash...), Nature Mints, Ability Capsule, Exp. Candies,
  newer Poke Balls, newer Berries, Gems, charms and more.
  The Linguicove Department Store's 2nd and 3rd floors are the
  big ones.
- Sam gives a MEGA STONE CASE with the Mega Ring. It holds every
  Mega Stone, so all Megas are available from the start.
- Kingambit (Leader's Crest) and Gholdengo (Gimmighoul Coins) can
  now be obtained. Both are at the Linguicove Department Store 3F.

Smaller changes
- Caught Pokemon are fully healed.
- Wonder Trade shows the right names in the trade scene.
- Prof. Alan's reward for the PASTALIA Dex is now Treecko, Torchic
  and Mudkip, and his regional forms call comes on your first
  visit to Macaroville.
- No more random calls from trainers.
- Feebas evolves into Milotic at level 35.
- Fiery Path is now PEPERONCINO PATH.
- Route 111's smashed rocks stay smashed.
- Gabby and Ty have a very important question about butter.
- WILDCARD'S CASTLE quiz has all-new questions.


PLEASE TRY
----------
- Switch BIKE modes with B, including at full speed. Try the
  white rails and bumpy slopes in ACRO mode, and the sandy slopes
  in FAST mode.
- If you already have a save: check your MEDICINE and TM pockets
  look right after loading.
- Search the Pokedex by typing a name.
- Talk to JASON in a few shops and buy some of the new stuff:
  a new TM, a held item, a Mint, a new Poke Ball.
- Use the MEGA STONE CASE to give a stone to a Pokemon.
- Walk around a route at different times of day.


DEBUG MENU
----------
This build has the developer's debug menus turned on, so you can
skip ahead or set up a test:
  - Overworld: hold R and press START (mGBA keyboard: hold S, Enter)
  - In battle: press SELECT on the battle menu (mGBA: Backspace)
  - Sprite viewer: SELECT on a summary screen (mGBA: Backspace)
Use a separate save or a save state first, and say in your report
if that save used the debug menu.


AFTER-BATTLE GLITCHES
---------------------
If anything odd happens right after a battle (the player moving on
their own, buttons not working, a freeze, strange graphics), make a
SAVE STATE right away (mGBA: File > Save State) and send it in.


KNOWN PLACEHOLDERS
------------------
- Brian, Prof. Alan, Sam, Mike, Joe Q, WILDCARD and JASON still use
  old Emerald sprites.
- Retro Salvage Games shops still have blue roofs and MART signs.
- The MEDICINE pocket shows the ITEMS bag picture.
- A few map graphics still say HOENN.
- Sam's shiny Metagross isn't cheddar-yellow yet.
